# The Ultimate Video Prompt Guide  
### For Veo, Video models, Social Media, Ads, Branding & Content Creation

---

## Core Principle (Video Prompting Rule #1)

**Describe what happens on screen — moment by moment.**

Video models don't just render visuals. They simulate:
- motion
- camera behavior
- pacing
- atmosphere
- sound (Veo 3+)

The clearer the **sequence**, the better the video.

---

Design prompts to be **clear, intentional, and brand-safe**.

---

## The Veo Prompt Framework (Use This Every Time)

Think in **cinematic layers**:

1. **Subject** – Who or what is on screen
2. **Action** – What is happening
3. **Context / Environment** – Where the scene takes place
4. **Style** – Film genre, animation style, realism level
5. **Camera** – Position, motion, framing
6. **Focus & Lens** – Depth of field, perspective
7. **Ambiance** – Lighting, color, mood
8. **Audio (Optional)** – Dialogue, SFX, ambient sound
9. **Aspect Ratio** – Platform-specific format

---

## Master Video Prompt Skeleton

A [shot type] of [subject] in [environment].
The subject is [action].
Style: [cinematic / animated / commercial / social].
Camera: [angle + movement].
Focus: [shallow / deep / macro / wide].
Lighting & color: [mood, tones].
Audio: [dialogue, sound effects, ambience].
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1].


---

## Video Prompting Basics (What Veo Understands Best)

### Subject
Be specific.
A desperate man in a weathered green trench coat


### Action
Motion matters.
He nervously dials a rotary phone and glances over his shoulder


### Style
Use film language.
Film noir, cinematic, gritty realism, high contrast black and white


### Camera & Composition (Optional but Powerful)
Close-up, dolly-in shot, eye-level, shallow depth of field


### Ambiance
Cool blue tones, neon lighting, rainy night atmosphere


---

## Prompting for Audio (Veo 3+)

### Dialogue
Use quotes.
Man (whispering): "This must be the key."


### Sound Effects (SFX)
Describe clearly.
Footsteps echoing, phone clicking, rain hitting pavement


### Ambient Noise
Set the soundscape.
A faint city hum and buzzing neon light in the background

## Social Media Video Prompts

### Instagram / TikTok Short
A vertical cinematic video of a confident entrepreneur working at a laptop in a modern café.
She types, pauses, and smiles with quiet confidence.
Style: clean, aspirational, social-media friendly.
Camera: slow push-in shot.
Lighting: warm natural light.
Mood: productive, inspiring.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.


---

### Brand Awareness Reel
A fast-paced montage video showcasing a modern brand.
Shots include close-ups of hands working, smiling customers, and clean product details.
Style: polished commercial, modern.
Camera: smooth transitions, dynamic cuts.
Music-driven pacing.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.


---

## Ad Video Prompts

### Paid Social Ad
A high-impact commercial-style video promoting a business service.
Opening shot hooks attention in the first 2 seconds.
Clear focus on the main benefit.
Style: bold, professional, conversion-focused.
Camera: crisp cuts, shallow depth of field.
Lighting: bright and clean.
Aspect ratio: 1:1 or 4:5.


---

### Product Ad
A cinematic product video showcasing a minimalist product on a clean surface.
The camera slowly rotates around the product.
Soft studio lighting highlights texture and quality.
Style: premium commercial.
Aspect ratio: 16:9.


---

## Landing Page Video Prompts

### Hero Section Background Video
A calm, cinematic background video for a website hero section.
Subject moves subtly to avoid distraction.
Soft lighting, slow camera movement.
Minimal visual noise.
Style: premium, modern, brand-safe.
Aspect ratio: 16:9.

## Business Branding Videos

### Brand Story Video
A cinematic brand story video showing behind-the-scenes moments.
Subjects include team collaboration, craftsmanship, and final results.
Style: authentic, documentary-inspired.

## Prompting with Reference Images (Image-to-Video)

Use images as the **first frame**.

Use the provided image as the opening frame.
Animate the subject naturally with realistic motion.
Preserve appearance, proportions, and lighting.
Add subtle camera movement and ambient sound.


Supports:
- Bringing illustrations to life
- Animating products
- Maintaining character consistency

---

## First & Last Frame Control (Advanced)

Start with the provided image as the opening frame.
End with the subject performing [final action].
Ensure smooth transition from start to finish.


Perfect for:
- Storytelling
- Visual punchlines
- Controlled narratives

---

## Extending Videos

Extend the video by continuing the existing motion naturally.
Maintain consistent lighting, pacing, and camera movement.


Note:
- Voice extension works best if dialogue exists in the final second.

---

## Negative Prompting (What NOT to Do)

❌ Don't say:
No buildings, don't add people


✅ Do say:
Natural landscape, open space, organic environment


Describe what *should* exist — not what shouldn't.

---

## Aspect Ratio Guide

- **16:9** → YouTube, websites, presentations
- **9:16** → TikTok, Reels, Shorts
- **1:1** → Instagram feed, ads

Always specify aspect ratio.

---

## Best Practices (Professional Level)

- Be cinematic, not technical
- Describe motion clearly
- Use film language
- Add emotion and intent
- Iterate: refine camera, lighting, pacing
- Think like a director, not a prompt engineer

---

## Known Limitations

- Generation latency varies
- Audio may occasionally fail due to safety filters
- Videos are temporarily stored
- AI watermarking is applied
- Some regions restrict person generation

---

## Final Rule

**If you can storyboard it in your head, Veo can generate it.**

Prompt like a director.
Design like a brand.
